# Forecasts and projections

`forecastThroughput` uses either a sample mean baseline or least-squares regression on actual acquisition timestamps. Defaults are a 120-second observation window, at least ten samples spanning thirty seconds, a 120-second horizon and a fifteen-minute maximum horizon. Configure defaults in `../config/forecastConfig.js` or edit forecast inputs in the panel. The controller uses speed-channel freshness and limits gaps by the accounting gap, forecast gap and telemetry freshness limits.

Only continuous valid observations in one source/device/calibration/input identity and scheduled-running regime qualify. Invalid or missing flow, stale telemetry, excessive gaps, source changes, zero-speed unknown state, operating-state changes and throughput-input edits reset the forecast window. New samples must qualify again. History remains provider-owned across navigation, is bounded by time and 2,048 entries, and is not restored over reload gaps. Other sensor updates and repeated render/subscription notifications do not add throughput samples.

Observed calculated flow is solid green; forecast flow is dashed amber. Forecasts carry source, method, quality, window, horizon and explicit continuation assumptions. The horizon starts at the latest acquisition time. Physical bounds are disabled by default; when configured, they clip the extrapolated curve and are disclosed. Design capacity is a reference and is never silently used as a bound. No confidence percentages, statistical bands or failure diagnoses are generated.

Data-driven shift projection adds accumulated observed tonnes to remaining scheduled hours times the average forecast rate over that remaining interval times user-assumed future uptime. It accounts for the age of the latest sample and requires that the entire future interval fit inside the selected forecast horizon. Otherwise it is unavailable. What-if projection uses a separate user-assumed future throughput; neither forecast values nor physical bounds overwrite that scenario. Expected throughput remains the loss-accounting baseline. Remaining shift hours are assumed scheduled when the existing Scheduled production period checkbox is selected; otherwise future scheduled hours are zero. Completed shifts show accumulated tonnes only, with no further extrapolation.

The optional temperature threshold extrapolation requires fresh qualified rising temperature data and a positive slope above the near-zero limit. Already-reached thresholds, stale data, insufficient history and excessive horizon have explicit states. It projects a temperature threshold at the current rate, never equipment failure time.
